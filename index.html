Part 1

Judging process

1.1Purpose

The Pit Showcase mirrors the real FTC judging process: portfolio, interview, pit visit, deliberation. We judge thinking, planning and teamwork, not a finished robot. Every team leaves with feedback to grow.

1.2Judging principles

Evidence over polish

Sketches, calculations, photos and logs count. A hand-drawn design with sound reasoning can beat a polished CAD with none.

Show it, don't promise it

Only work already done, or plans backed by a document (budget, letter, dated timeline), earn points.

Judge the journey

Scores reflect what a team did with the time, people and resources it had.

Same criteria for all

Teams are placed in two cohorts (A and B) with separate podiums. Rubrics, questions and standards are identical.

Encourage first

Judges ask, listen and guide. The goal is learning, not ranking.

1.4Documentation

Each team submits a 1-page poster and a 2-page document before the event. Together they work like an FTC engineering portfolio: judges read both before the interview and use them for every award.

Table 1-3: 2-page document

SectionWhat to include

Note: Optional but strongly encouraged. They are not scored on their own, and neat presentation earns no extra points. Clear, honest content is what helps judges see the team's work.

2.2.1Best Overall Team Inspire1 winner, all teamsConsensus

The team that best shows the FTC way: strong in design and strategy, connected to its community, well organized, and working as one team. Judges weigh the poster, document, team interview, Mystery Mission and pit visit together, and decide by consensus. No points are added up.

Table 2-2: Best Overall Team criteria

Best Overall Team Criteria
Required1Team is shortlisted for at least two awards.
Required2Every member can explain the team's robot idea and journey in their own words.
Encouraged3Design and strategy are clearly connected to each other.
Encouraged4The team shows real community connection and a plan to continue.
Encouraged5Roles are shared and the team works and communicates as one.
Encouraged6Poster, document, interview and pit tell the same story.
Disclaimer: People's Choice results are not considered for Best Overall.

2.2.4Problem Solver 1 winner per cohort15 points

For the team that thinks best under pressure. Decided only by the Mystery Mission, a surprise challenge teams are not told about in advance. Each team gets its own unique problem (for example, a robot that loses points in endgame, or a limited budget for a new mechanism), so answers cannot be shared. Run by a separate 2-judge panel. No pit interview needed.

Table 2-6: Mystery Mission format (15 minutes per team)

StepTimeWhat happens
Brief3 minJudges read the problem aloud.
Think and ask6 minThe team asks questions. Judges answer from a prepared data card and only what is asked.
Present6 minThe team explains its breakdown and solution, with a sketch, numbers or reasoning as proof.

Table 2-7: Problem Solver criteria

Problem Solver Criteria
Required1Questions asked are relevant and dig for facts.0-3
Required2The problem is broken into clear parts.0-3
Encouraged3The solution follows from the analysis.0-3
Encouraged4Proof: numbers, a sketch or a test idea back the solution.0-3
Encouraged5The team shares roles and ideas, with everyone contributing.0-3
Note: The Mystery Mission score is also considered in the Best Overall discussion.
2.2.5People's Choice Motivate1 winner, all teamsVote

For the team that best brings energy and welcome to the event. The winner is chosen by vote. The voting method will be decided by the organizers on the day, and no judge scores apply.

Disclaimer: People's Choice is not considered for Best Overall.

2.3Eligibility and award rules

Table 2-9: Award rules

RuleDetail
HierarchyAwards are decided in this order: Best Overall, Engineering Excellence, Team Impact, Problem Solver. A team wins at most one of these, so recognition is spread across teams.
People's ChoiceSeparate. It can go to any team, including one that wins another award. It is not considered for Best Overall.
CohortsEngineering Excellence and Team Impact each have a 1st, 2nd and 3rd place in Cohort A and in Cohort B. Problem Solver has one winner per cohort. Best Overall and People's Choice are open to all teams.
